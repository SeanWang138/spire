# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 14 (App Router), React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Three.js, near-api-js.

## Users

Web3 GameFi players, DeFi/NEAR raiders, on-chain gaming enthusiasts, and backer yield seekers engaging with real NEAR smart contracts.

## Product Purpose

SpireFi is an autonomous Dark-Fantasy Tactical GameFi protocol on NEAR Protocol. It delivers high-stakes, signless combat against escalating boss floors with real-time adaptive elemental resistance, a circular tokenless ticket/bounty economy, and TEE AI verification.

## Positioning

Truly signless, deterministic on-chain combat with 3D citadel spatial immersion, mathematical resistance shifting, and verifiable boss AI—distinct from generic clicker web3 apps and flat DeFi dashboards.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile browsers accessing NEAR testnet. Users review live boss telemetry, execute 3-turn combat combos signlessly via Function Call Access Keys, gather materials, forge weapons, trade on a bonding curve, and back floor vaults.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three boss floors with unique mechanics: Floor 1 (Foundation), Floor 2 (Frost Ward), Floor 3 (Enrage + NEP-171 Conqueror Trophy mint).
- 3 combat elements (Solar, Frost, Void) with strict 7,000 bps Tribute confiscation.
- Deterministic ticket splits (90% damage, 10% killing blow; jackpot, backer, buyback, merchant reserve).
- Real WebGL 3D citadel visualization with full keyboard, mouse, and touch accessibility.
- Zero fake data, zero uninstructed custom raster assets, full dark mode obsidian aesthetic.

## Brand Commitments

- Primary palette: Solar Amber (`#F59E0B`), Frost Cyan (`#38BDF8`), Void Violet (`#A855F7`), Jackpot Emerald (`#00EC97`), HP Crimson (`#EF4444`).
- Material: Carved obsidian basalt (`#080B11`, `#0F1424`, `#13192B`, `#161D33`) with gilded/elemental edge highlights and directional lighting.
- Typography: Serif display (Cinzel / Classic Serif), Clean Sans, UI Monospace with Tabular Figures.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified NEAR testnet smart contract (`spire.testnet`).
- Canonical mathematical models and tests in `src/lib/simulation/`.
- Documented protocol specs in `09-games.md` and `PROJECT.md`.

## Product Principles

1. **Obsidian Depth Over Flat Cards:** Surfaces feel carved and dimensional, not generic SaaS cards.
2. **Deterministic Mechanical Honesty:** Visuals directly reflect live contract state (HP, resistance, tickets, pools).
3. **Immersive Yet Accessible:** 3D graphics enhance immersion while guaranteeing touch, keyboard, and reduced-motion parity.
4. **Signless Velocity:** Zero wallet popup friction during tactical combat turns.
